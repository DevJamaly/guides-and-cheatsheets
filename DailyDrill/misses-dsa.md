> Append the "misses block" Claude outputs at the end of each session.
> Format: `- [YYYY-MM-DD] D-bucket — specific gap (type: …; difficulty: …)`
>
> Types: `pattern` (misrecognized / wrong tool) · `boundary` (off-by-one,
> loop bounds, pointer math) · `complexity` (claim vs. reality) · `edge`
> (missed empty/single/duplicate/overflow) · `timebox` (correct but over
> time) · `hint-assisted` (solved, but not alone).
>
> `(TEACH FIRST)` = the same mechanism failed 2+ times. It gets explained at
> the start of the next session, retested the session after, never turned
> straight back into a problem.
>
> Monthly: delete anything cleared 3+ times. A weakness log that only grows
> stops being a signal.
